Excavators and Trenching

Excavators are used for digging, trenching and moving material. Most excavator injuries come from trench collapses, struck-by hazards in the swing radius, rollovers and hitting buried utilities.

Training required

  • Hands-on training on the specific machine, with a qualified operator, before working alone
  • Read and follow the manufacturer's operator manual for that model
  • A competent person must inspect trenches and excavations daily and after rain or other changes (OSHA 1926.651)
  • Workers in trenches must be trained to recognize cave-in hazards

Before you dig

  • Call 811 so underground utilities are marked before digging
  • Do a walk-around pre-use inspection: tracks, hydraulic lines, bucket and attachments, leaks
  • Use shoring, shielding or sloping in trenches 5 feet deep or more
  • Keep spoil piles and equipment at least 2 feet back from the trench edge
  • Provide a ladder or ramp within 25 feet of workers in trenches 4 feet deep or more
  • Barricade the swing radius, wear your seatbelt and keep a spotter when visibility is limited

Official instructions and resources

  • OSHA excavation standard (29 CFR 1926 Subpart P)
  • OSHA trenching and excavation safety

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions, your company's procedures and the full OSHA standard. If you are unsure, stop and ask your foreman or safety professional.
