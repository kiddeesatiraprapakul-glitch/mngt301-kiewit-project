OSHA Safety Training

OSHA's Outreach Training Program gives construction workers a foundation in recognizing and avoiding common job site hazards.

Outreach courses

  • OSHA 10-hour Construction: for entry-level workers
  • OSHA 30-hour Construction: for supervisors and workers with safety responsibility
  • Courses are taught by OSHA-authorized trainers, in person or online
  • Many employers, unions and some states require one of these cards on site

Know the Focus Four hazards

  • Falls
  • Struck-by hazards (moving equipment, falling objects)
  • Caught-in or between hazards (trench collapse, pinch points)
  • Electrocution

Official instructions and resources

  • OSHA Outreach Training Program
  • OSHA training home
  • OSHA construction industry resources

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions, your company's procedures and the full OSHA standard. If you are unsure, stop and ask your foreman or safety professional.
